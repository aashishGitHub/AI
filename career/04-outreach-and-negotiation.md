## 6. Compensation (plan Q3 + Q10)
- **Never give your current CTC first.** India has no ban on asking for salary history, so expect to be asked for payslips later.
- Script: "I'd rather anchor on the role. For [Level] at [Company] in Bengaluru, market data puts total comp around [Levels.fyi median]. I'm expecting something in that range, depending on the level and the mix."
- Medians from the plan (Levels.fyi, self-reported, Oct–Nov 2026 — **recheck before quoting**): Amazon SDE III ~₹1.36Cr · Google L5 ~₹1.26Cr · Atlassian P50 ~₹1.12Cr · Microsoft 64 ~₹98L · Microsoft 63 ~₹71.5L · Salesforce SMTS ~₹77.3L · Bengaluru Senior SWE overall ~₹56.2L.
- **Confirm the level before the onsite.** Down-levelling is the real risk.
- Compare **year-1 cash** against current fixed pay. RSUs are 30–45% of TC and Amazon vests back-loaded.
- Notice period: check your contract (often 60–90 days). Ask if they buy out notice. Negotiate early release only after a signed offer.
- Run offers in parallel; let competing offers, not your history, set the number.

## 7. If a layoff conversation happens (plan Q10 — not legal advice; verify with a lawyer)
Ask for in writing: severance in months, garden leave, RSU acceleration or pro-ration, bonus pro-ration, leave encashment, extended health insurance, a clean relieving letter, and "resignation" vs "termination" wording. **Don't sign the same day.**

## How these connect
- **01 is the hub:** fill its STAR skeleton for 6-8 stories first; every other question in this round draws from that bank.
- **One migration story, many uses:** 02 (migration) reuses round 13's strangler-fig mechanics (13-03); the same story can answer 05 (debt) and 08 (influence) if it included cross-team adoption.
- **Persuasion cluster:** 03 (disagreement, peer level), 06 (RFC, org level) and 08 (influence, no authority) share one pattern: understand constraints, agree criteria, evidence/prototype, explicit decision, commit, make adoption cheap.
- **Multiplier cluster:** 04 (mentoring) and 07 (incident) both end in systemic mechanisms (docs, lint rules, alerts) rather than personal heroics; that is the staff-level signal.
- **Suggested order:** 01 -> 07 -> 03 -> 04 -> 05 -> 02 -> 08 -> 06.
